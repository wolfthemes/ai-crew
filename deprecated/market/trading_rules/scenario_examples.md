# Trading Scenario Examples

## Scenario 1: Bullish FVG Setup
### Market Context
- Daily trend is bullish with higher highs and higher lows
- Recent pullback created a Fair Value Gap on H4 timeframe
- M30 shows a change in state of delivery (CiSD) from bearish to bullish
- Approaching London session open

### Entry Conditions
1. Price retraces into the bullish FVG zone
2. M30 shows evidence of buyers stepping in (bullish engulfing or hammer)
3. M5 confirms with a CiSD
4. Entry at market after confirmation candle closes

### Trade Management
- _Set and forget_

## Scenario 2: Bearish Break of Structure
### Market Context
- Daily trend has been ranging for several several days
- Price just broke below external range low and the previous daily candle closed below
- M30 shows a CiSD during/after Asian Session
- M5 shows a mitigation block at the M30 CiSD level

### Entry Conditions
1. Wait for a pullback to retest the broken structure
2. M30 shows rejection at previous support (now resistance)
3. M5 shows bearish continuation pattern
4. Entry on close of confirmation candle

### Trade Management
- _Always Set and forget_
