# Fundamental Context Analysis

## Key Economic Indicators to Monitor
- **Interest Rate Differentials**: Fed vs ECB rate expectations
- **Inflation Metrics**: CPI US vs HICP Europe
- **Employment Data**: NFP, unemployment rates
- **PMI**: Manufacturing and services PMI for US and Europe
- **GDP Growth**: Relative economic growth rates
- **Market Sentiment**: Risk-on/Risk-off conditions

## Critical Correlations
- **EUR/USD vs DXY (Dollar Index)**: Strong negative correlation
- **EUR/USD vs GBP/USD**: Strong positive correlation (typically)
- **EUR/USD vs US-Europe yield spread**: Widening spreads often negative for EUR
- **EUR/USD vs equity markets**: Varies, but often tracks risk sentiment

## Economic Cycle Impact
- **Expansion Phases**: Monitor relative growth rates
- **Contraction/Recession**: Typically favors USD (safe haven)
- **Monetary Policy Divergence**: Focus on relative policy stances
- **Fiscal Policy Differences**: Government spending, debt levels

## Interpreting News Impact
- **Surprise Factor**: Deviation from consensus expectations
- **Market Positioning**: Crowded positions amplify news impact
- **Central Bank Communication**: Forward guidance often more important than actual data
- **Temporal Impact**: Some news has short-lived effect, others shift longer-term trends

## News Trading Guidelines
- **Avoid Trading During High-Impact Releases**: Stay out 15 minutes before/after
- **Focus on Second-Order Effects**: How data affects rate expectations
- **Watch for Fade Opportunities**: Extreme reactions often reverse
- **News Confirmation**: Only enter if fundamentals align with technical setup
