# Operational Checklists

## Pre-Session Checklist
- [ ] **Calendar Check**: Verify no major economic releases during London session
- [ ] **Overnight Analysis**: Review Asian session price action and any significant moves
- [ ] **Multi-Timeframe Review**: Systematically analyze W1 → D1 → H4 → M30
- [ ] **ERL/IRL Liquidity Zones**: Identify key levels active for today's session
- [ ] **Bias Determination**: Establish directional bias based on HTF analysis
- [ ] **Correlation Check**: Review correlated pairs (GBP/USD) and DXY
- [ ] **News Impact**: Assess impact of any overnight news on EUR/USD
- [ ] **Previous Day Close**: Note the close price and daily structure

## Trade Entry Checklist
- [ ] **Time Window**: Confirm within 9:30-11:00 Paris time (±10min)
- [ ] **Trading Day**: Confirm it is Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday
- [ ] **HTF Alignment**: Verify trade direction aligns with higher timeframe trend
- [ ] **Valid Setup**: Confirm one of our approved setup types is present
- [ ] **Structure Confirmation**: Verify CiSD on M30 and M5
- [ ] **Entry Confirmation**: Verify CiSD on M5 above M30 CSID 50% level
- [ ] **Risk Assessment**: Calculate position size based on 1% risk rule
- [ ] **RRR Check**: Verify minimum 1:2 risk-reward ratio
- [ ] **Clear Invalidation**: Define precise invalidation point for stop loss
- [ ] **Multiple Confirmations**: Ensure at least 3 confluent factors support trade

## Trade Management Checklist
- [ ] **No interfering**: Do not interfere with the trade by moving the stop loss or closing manually
- [ ] **Scaling Plan**: Consider adding to position only if risk is already mitigated
- [ ] **Trade Journal Entry**: Create preliminary journal entry before trade execution
- [ ] **Mental State**: Assess emotional state before entering trade
- [ ] **Max Exposure**: Verify not exceeding maximum permitted open risk

## Post-Trade Analysis Checklist
- [ ] **Rule Adherence**: Note any trading rules that were broken
- [ ] **Emotional Control**: Assess emotional management during trade
- [ ] **Lessons Learned**: Document key takeaways from this trade
- [ ] **Improvement Areas**: Identify specific areas for improvement
- [ ] **Journal Update**: Complete full trade journal entry with screenshots
