# EUR/USD Weekly Market Report – 2025-04-21

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The EUR/USD pair experienced heightened volatility over the past week, driven by a confluence of disappointing Eurozone data, robust US economic releases, and dovish signals from the European Central Bank (ECB). The euro struggled to maintain upward momentum, succumbing to renewed selling pressure as German Manufacturing PMI and Eurozone CPI both missed expectations, while US consumer confidence and payrolls data outperformed. The ECB’s decision to keep rates unchanged at 3.75% was accompanied by a notably dovish tone, fueling speculation of potential rate cuts in the coming months. In contrast, the Federal Reserve maintained its hawkish stance, underpinned by persistent inflation and labor market strength.

EUR/USD opened the week near 1.0750, briefly tested resistance at 1.0800, but ultimately retreated to test the critical 1.0700 support multiple times, with a weekly low at 1.0675. Technicals confirm a bearish bias, with momentum indicators and price action favoring further downside. Sentiment is decisively bearish across institutional, social, and retail channels, though positioning is not yet at reversal extremes.

Looking ahead, key US inflation and labor market data, along with ECB President Lagarde’s upcoming speech, are likely to drive further volatility. The path of least resistance remains to the downside, with 1.0700/1.0675 as pivotal support levels. Only a material shift in ECB or Fed policy, or a significant upside surprise in Eurozone data, would alter the bearish outlook in the near term.

## 4. Fundamental Analysis

### Monetary Policy Comparison

- **ECB:** Maintained rates at 3.75% (Apr 17), with a dovish tone. Lagarde emphasized subdued inflation and growth risks. Market expects potential rate cuts as soon as June/July if inflation remains soft.
- **Fed:** Holds rates at 5.50%, maintains hawkish stance. Strong US data and sticky inflation justify “higher for longer” policy. First rate cut not expected until late Q3/Q4 2025.
- **Rate Differential:** US-EU spread at 175bps in favor of USD, a key driver of EUR/USD downside. Forward guidance and OIS/futures pricing reinforce this gap.
- **Balance Sheet:** Fed’s QT outpaces ECB’s, supporting USD.

### Economic Data Analysis

- **GDP Growth:** US Q1 2025 tracking ~2.1% annualized; Eurozone Q1 ~0.2% YoY. US outperformance is clear.
- **Inflation:** Eurozone CPI YoY at 2.4% (disinflation trend); US Core PCE expected at 0.3% MoM, YoY >2.5%. US inflation stickier, supporting Fed hawkishness.
- **Employment:** US NFP at 182K (Apr), unemployment ~3.8%; Eurozone unemployment 6.4%, job creation lagging.
- **Manufacturing/Services:** German PMI in contraction (47.8); US ISM PMIs in expansion (>50).
- **Consumer Confidence:** US at 105.2 (strong); Eurozone subdued.

### Geopolitical Factors

- **Eurozone:** Russia-Ukraine conflict, EU political fragmentation, trade tensions with China, and Brexit-related issues weigh on sentiment.
- **US:** Political risks (election cycle, fiscal debates) present but outweighed by global demand for USD assets.
- **Trade:** US-EU friction persists, but capital flows favor USD.
- **Political Stability:** Eurozone faces more immediate risk (EU elections, government instability).

### Flow Analysis

- **Capital Flows:** Portfolio flows favor US Treasuries/equities; Eurozone outflows notable from German/French bonds.
- **Trade Balance:** US trade deficit offset by capital inflows; Eurozone current account surplus narrows.
- **Central Bank Reserves:** Modest reduction in euro allocations, favoring USD/gold.

### Fundamental Outlook

- **Short-Term (1-2 Weeks):** Bearish bias. US data likely to reinforce USD strength; ECB rhetoric likely dovish. EUR/USD to test/break 1.0700/1.0675, targeting 1.0630.
- **Medium-Term (1-3 Months):** Negative for euro. Widening rate differential, US macro outperformance, and capital flows into USD assets to keep EUR/USD under pressure. ECB cut risk in early summer; only a major upside surprise in Eurozone data or dovish Fed pivot would alter trajectory.

**Key Drivers:**
- US-EU rate differential
- Relative growth/inflation
- Capital flows
- Geopolitical risk premium
- ECB vs Fed guidance

**Potential Price Targets:**
- **Short-term:** `1.0675` (support), `1.0630` (next target)
- **Medium-term:** `1.0580` (extended downside)
- **Upside risk:** Limited to `1.0760-1.0800` unless fundamentals shift

**Conclusion:**
EUR/USD fundamentals are decisively bearish, driven by dovish ECB, resilient US data, and a widening rate differential. The pair is likely to remain under pressure, with 1.0700/1.0675 as key supports. A break lower opens the door to 1.0630 and potentially 1.0580 in the coming weeks. Only a significant shift in ECB or Fed policy, or a surprise improvement in Eurozone data, would alter the bearish outlook.
