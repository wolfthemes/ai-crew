**Rationale:**  
The narrowing policy gap, persistent Eurozone inflation, and easing US/EU trade tensions support EUR/USD on dips. However, the USD retains a yield advantage and could rebound sharply if US data surprises to the upside or if the Fed signals renewed hawkishness.
