# Technical Patterns and Structures

## Candlestick Patterns
### Bullish Patterns
- **Hammer**: Long lower shadow, small body at the top
- **Bullish Engulfing**: Larger bullish candle engulfs previous bearish candle
- **Morning Star**: Three-candle pattern showing reversal from downtrend
- **Piercing Line**: Bullish candle closes above midpoint of previous bearish candle

### Bearish Patterns
- **Shooting Star**: Long upper shadow, small body at the bottom
- **Bearish Engulfing**: Larger bearish candle engulfs previous bullish candle
- **Evening Star**: Three-candle pattern showing reversal from uptrend
- **Dark Cloud Cover**: Bearish candle closes below midpoint of previous bullish candle

## Market Structure Elements
### Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
- **Bullish FVG**: When current candle's low is higher than previous candle's high
- **Bearish FVG**: When current candle's high is lower than previous candle's low
- **Significance**: Larger gaps have higher probability of being filled
- **Order**: Newer FVGs are typically filled before older ones

### Change in Structure Direction (CiSD)
- **Bullish Structure**: Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL)
- **Bearish Structure**: Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL)
- **Consolidation**: When structure is unclear or ranging
- **Break of Structure (BOS)**: When prior structure is violated, signaling potential reversal

### Order Blocks (OB)
- **Bullish OB**: Last bearish candle before a bullish move
- **Bearish OB**: Last bullish candle before a bearish move
- **Identification**: Focus on the body of the candle, not the wicks
- **Mitigation**: Price returning to an order block often leads to reaction

## Multi-Timeframe Analysis Framework
1. **Weekly/Daily**: Establish trend direction and major S/R levels
2. **H4/H1**: Identify key swing points and potential order blocks
3. **M30**: Confirm market structure changes (CiSD)
4. **M5**: Fine-tune entry with precise price action patterns

## Volume Analysis
- **Volume Confirmation**: Price moves with higher volume have more significance
- **Volume Divergence**: Decreasing volume on price moves suggests potential reversal
- **Volume at Key Levels**: High volume at support/resistance increases importance
