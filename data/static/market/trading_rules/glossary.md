# Trading System Glossary
- **CiSD**: Change in State of Delivery - when market structure shifts from bearish to bullish or vice versa
- **FVG**: Fair Value Gap - inefficiency in price where a strong move leaves an unfilled gap
- **OB**: Order Block - the last opposing candle before a significant move
- **SMT Divergence**: Smart Money Technique Divergence - divergences of price between two correlated (or uncorrelated) assets.

## Risk Management Terms
- **R:R**: Risk to Reward Ratio - comparison of potential loss to potential gain
- **BE**: Break Even - moving stop loss to entry point to eliminate risk
- **DD**: Drawdown - decline in account value from peak
- **RR**: Risk Reward - usually expressed as a ratio (1:2, 1:3)
- **PS**: Position Size - amount of capital allocated to a trade
- **TP**: Take Profit - predetermined price target
- **SL**: Stop Loss - predetermined exit point to limit losses

## Session and Timeframe Terms
- **LTF**: Lower Timeframe - shorter term charts (M1, M3, M5)
- **MTF**: Medium Timeframe - medium term charts (M15, M30, H1)
- **HTF**: Higher Timeframe - longer term charts (H4, D1, W1)
- **LS**: London Session - trading hours when London markets are active
- **NY**: New York Session - trading hours when US markets are active
