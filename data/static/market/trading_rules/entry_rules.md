# Entry Rules

## Prerequisites
- Prior identification of the trend on HTF (Weekly/Daily)
- Verification of the economic calendar (no major news)
- Active London session (9:30-11:00 Paris time, ±10min)

## Level 1: Weekly/Daily Analysis
- Identify the direction of the main trend
- Locate liquidity zones
- Identify Fair Value Gaps (FVG) on D1 and H4
- Spot potential liquidity grab areas

## Level 2: M30 Analysis
- Confirm market structure (CiSD) on M30
- Identify retracements in the trend
- Validate FVGs as potential entry zones
- Monitor liquidity grabs (wicks)

## Level 3: M5 Analysis
- Final confirmation with CiSD on M5
- Precise identification of entry point
- Search for reversal candlestick patterns

## Authorized Entry Modes
1. **FVG Mode**:
   - Entry on partial filling of an FVG aligned with the trend
   - Confirmation by reversal pattern on M5

2. **Liquidity Grab Mode**:
   - Entry after an HTF liquidity grab
   - Confirmation by price return to structural zone

3. **BOS/CHoCH Mode**:
   - Break of structure on M30 confirmed
   - Pullback to the breakout zone
