Weekly profile

Wednesday’s early action has failed to break out of this range, further validating the pattern. According to the Weekly Profile Guide, Wednesday in a Consolidation Reversal week typically features a false breakout or stop-hunt, often triggered by scheduled news, followed by a reversal and a true directional move later in the week. The current week’s range is notably narrower than last week’s (1.1450–1.1350), and the probability of a late-week reversal or breakout is elevated. Key levels to monitor are 1.1425 (resistance) and 1.1380 (support); a sustained move beyond these would signal a genuine expansion, but the first breakout is likely to be false.
