EUR/USD Weekly Market Report – 2025-04-22

Executive Summary

The EUR/USD pair experienced a dramatic surge over the past week, breaking decisively above the psychologically significant 1.1500 level for the first time since November 2021. This move was driven by a confluence of fundamental and technical factors, most notably the European Central Bank’s (ECB) dovish rate cut on April 17 and a sharp US Dollar sell-off following President Trump’s public criticism of the Federal Reserve’s independence. Despite the ECB’s easing, the Euro displayed remarkable resilience, buoyed by market confidence in the central bank’s credibility and a rotation away from the USD amid heightened US political risk.

Technically, EUR/USD staged a textbook bullish breakout, with momentum indicators confirming trend acceleration but also warning of overbought conditions. Sentiment is overwhelmingly bullish among institutional and speculative traders, while retail traders are heavily short—a classic contrarian signal that supports further upside in the near term.

Looking ahead, the upcoming week’s high-impact PMI releases for both the Eurozone and the US (April 23) are poised to inject fresh volatility. While the medium-term outlook remains bullish, traders should be alert to the risk of a short-term pullback if sentiment becomes excessively stretched or if economic data disappoints. Overall, the path of least resistance remains higher, with 1.1620–1.1700 as plausible targets, provided 1.1400 support holds.

  • The ECB cut its deposit rate by 25bps to 2.75%, maintaining a dovish, data-dependent stance. Despite the easing, the Euro rallied, reflecting market confidence in the ECB’s ability to manage downside risks without destabilizing the currency.

US Dollar Sell-Off and Political Pressure on the Fed (April 22, 2025):

  • President Trump’s public attacks on Fed Chair Powell and calls for rate cuts raised concerns about the Fed’s independence, triggering a sharp USD sell-off.
  • The political uncertainty undermined the USD’s safe-haven appeal and fueled a rotation into the Euro and other G10 currencies.

Market Reaction:

  • EUR/USD surged past 1.1500, gaining over 1% on a daily basis at times. The rally was exacerbated by thin holiday liquidity and the absence of major resistance levels until 1.20.
  • The Euro’s resilience, despite ECB easing, was a key surprise, highlighting the importance of relative policy credibility and political risk in driving FX markets.

Economic Data Releases:

  • No major surprises in economic data; market focus was squarely on central bank communications and political developments.
  • Both US and Eurozone data were largely in line with expectations, with no significant impact on EUR/USD.

Technical Analysis

Price Action Analysis

Over the past week, EUR/USD staged a decisive bullish breakout, surging from the 1.1380 region to highs above 1.1550. The rally was catalyzed by the ECB’s dovish rate cut and accelerated by a sharp US Dollar sell-off following political pressure on the Fed. Price action featured strong bullish daily candles, including a Marubozu bar on April 17, confirming aggressive buying interest.

Key Price Levels Tested/Broken:

  • 1.1400: Clean break on April 17, with strong bullish momentum.
  • 1.1500: Psychological and multi-year resistance, decisively breached on April 22.

Candlestick Patterns:

  • April 18–21: Series of small-bodied candles (dojis/spinning tops) near 1.1500, indicating indecision and consolidation.
  • April 22: Bullish engulfing candle on the 4H chart, signaling renewed upside momentum.

Fundamental Analysis

ECB:

  • Balance sheet shrinking at a measured pace; no new QE.
  • Market confidence in ECB’s ability to manage downside risks.

Fed:

  • Policy rate steady at 4.75%, but under intense political pressure.
  • President Trump’s attacks on Fed independence have raised concerns about policy credibility.
  • Slower pace of quantitative tightening; forward guidance ambiguous.

Interest Rate Differentials:

  • EUR/USD rate differential has narrowed to ~200bps, lowest since 2021.
  • Markets price a 60% chance of a Fed rate cut by June 2025; ECB expected to pause further cuts.
  • Narrowing differential is a key driver of EUR/USD strength.
